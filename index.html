What is databasekontaktu.cz?

databasekontaktu.cz is the largest database of company contacts in the Czech Republic. It unifies the identity of companies from official registers (company number, address, industry, statutory officers) with contacts from public sources (e-mail, phone, website) into one profile, in which you can search for companies, filter by industry and export.
